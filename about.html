Our Story

About Cairo

Cairo Restaurant was born from a simple belief — that Zaria deserves a dining experience that celebrates flavour, community, and culture without compromise. Founded by a woman who poured her passion for food into every detail, Cairo quickly became a beloved landmark on Zaria Sokoto Road.

We serve halal cuisine crafted from fresh, locally sourced ingredients. From hearty morning breakfasts to late-night cocktails, our menu reflects the warmth and richness of Northern Nigerian hospitality blended with international culinary flair.

Every dish is made with intention. Every visit is treated as a privilege. Whether you're here for a solo lunch, a family celebration, or a casual drive-through — Cairo welcomes you.
